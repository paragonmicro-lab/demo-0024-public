# Get help

- **In class:** ask your **instructor**. Stuck for five minutes means ask; nobody is graded on getting stuck. They can
  reset your login, catch you up, or show any step on the big screen.
- **A step fails:** every section has a 🎬 recording of the same steps. Watch it while the instructor looks at your
  lab. A step marked **[verify]** is still being proven: tell the instructor rather than improvising.
- **Your login doesn't work:** your instructor can reset it. Don't keep retrying: repeated wrong tries can lock the
  account for a while.
- **A page is stuck after a timeout:** sign in again in a **private window**. Nothing is lost: your VM, your data and
  your terminal live in the lab, not in the browser.
- **After class:** the lab team, at [lab@paragonmicro.com](mailto:lab@paragonmicro.com).

This site is public and never contains passwords or anything about your company's environment.
